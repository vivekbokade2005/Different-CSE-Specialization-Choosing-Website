    About
    
      
    This tool is a student-focused interactive roadmap. It uses YouTube-first resources and stores progress locally (no server). Use the quiz, choose a domain, mark progress and take notes.
    
    
    
  
    
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Help & Support
    
      
    
        
    
          
    ❓ How to use Roadmaps?
    
          
    Choose a domain from the navigation or quiz, then expand steps to explore resources and mark progress.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    📝 Where are my notes stored?
    
          
    Your notes and progress are saved locally in your browser. No server or login is needed.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    ⚙️ Having issues?
    
          
    Try refreshing the page. If problems continue, clear browser localStorage and restart.
